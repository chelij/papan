# Demo artwork

These screenshots show the real Papan app with a temporary, documentation-only collection. No artwork or sample library is installed with Papan.

All artwork below is marked public domain in [The Met Collection API](https://metmuseum.github.io/), whose Open Access images and data are offered under CC0. Metadata was retrieved on 29 September 2026. The museum does not endorse Papan.

- [Wheat Field with Cypresses](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436535) — Vincent van Gogh, 1889. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [Self-Portrait with a Straw Hat (obverse: The Potato Peeler)](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436532) — Vincent van Gogh, 1887. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [Irises](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436528) — Vincent van Gogh, 1890. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [Oleanders](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436530) — Vincent van Gogh, 1888. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [Shoes](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436533) — Vincent van Gogh, 1888. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [The Death of Socrates](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436105) — Jacques Louis David, 1787. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [James-Jacques-Joseph Tissot (1836–1902)](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436144) — Edgar Degas, ca. 1867–68. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [Mars and Venus United by Love](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437891) — Paolo Veronese (Paolo Caliari), 1570s. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- [1807, Friedland](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437052) — Ernest Meissonier, ca. 1861–75. The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

Run `npm run demo` to recreate the screenshots. It downloads the credited images into ignored `artifacts/demo/`, builds an isolated temporary library, captures the app, and deletes that library.
