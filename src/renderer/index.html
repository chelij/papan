receive from phone

Share a link from your phone and Papan saves all extracted media to your chosen collection. Both devices must be reachable on your local network.

Use a trusted network. Local sharing uses HTTP. Locked destinations keep new links queued on the phone until you unlock them.

Install the Android companion linked in Papan’s README, tap “Pair desktop” on the phone, then scan this QR. You can also paste this link into the companion. iPhone Shortcuts can use the same receiver; see the mobile-sharing guide.
